# Moca company scoring guide, version 1

Score how good a fit a company is for Moca, from 1 to 5. The score is about the company, never about a person. Use only the information given. If the information is too thin to judge, give at most 2 and say what is missing.

## Signals that raise the score

* UK owner or fund that holds commercial buildings with several tenants (multi let offices, retail, industrial or mixed use).
* Funds or asset managers with EPC, MEES or CRREM risk: older stock, low EPC ratings, leases ending soon, or public net zero targets.
* Managing agents (property managers) that compete for management tenders, bill tenants for energy or service charges, or want new services and income.
* Occupiers (tenants) with many UK sites, especially 20 or more, or with ESOS or SECR reporting duties, or energy heavy sites.
* A large UK portfolio (for example, more than 500,000 sq ft or more than 10 buildings).
* Recent signs of change: a new ESG or energy hire, an acquisition, a fund raise, building work, a tender, or new rules that affect them.
* Limits on power supply from the grid affecting their sites or plans.

## Signals that lower the score

* Mostly residential property, with little or no commercial property.
* Mostly outside the UK.
* A single small site, or no property at all.
* No link to energy, carbon or building value that Moca could help with.

## Score meanings

* 5: Strong fit. Clearly in one of Moca's three customer groups in the UK, with at least two of the signals that raise the score.
* 4: Good fit. Clearly in one customer group in the UK, with one strong signal.
* 3: Possible fit. Some link to UK commercial property or energy, but size, location or need is unclear.
* 2: Weak fit, or not enough information to judge.
* 1: Poor fit. No link to UK commercial property or energy.

## Customer groups

* ASSET_ESG: asset and ESG managers, funds and investors that own buildings.
* PROPERTY_MANAGER: managing agents that run buildings for owners.
* OCCUPIER: tenants that occupy buildings, especially across several sites.
* UNSURE: when the information does not make the group clear.
