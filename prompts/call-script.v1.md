## Your task

You are given a starting call script, the reason for the call, facts about the company, the person's job title, and possibly a recent news item. Write:

* opening: one or two sentences that say who is calling, from Moca, and why this company specifically. Ask for permission to continue.
* questions: exactly three open questions that help understand their situation.
* objections: two to four likely objections, each with a short, honest reply.
* ask: one clear request at the end, such as a meeting of a stated length.
