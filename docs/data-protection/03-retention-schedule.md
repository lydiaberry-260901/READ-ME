# Retention schedule

**Draft for legal review.** The periods marked "setting" can be changed by an admin in the CRM (Privacy centre, Keeping data). Update this schedule when they change.

Each month (on the 1st) the CRM lists records past their period. **Nothing is deleted until an admin approves the list.** Contacts on open deals, or with a request about their data in progress, are never listed. Each record is checked again when the list is approved, so anything used in the meantime is kept.

| Data | Kept for | Then | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business contacts (name, job title, work email and phone, company, notes, source) | 24 months after the last activity (setting) | Deleted with their emails, transcripts, drafts and tasks. Calls and meetings stay in totals without anything identifying them | Long enough for typical sales cycles in commercial property |
| Emails with contacts (addresses, subject, short extract; full text only if sent from the CRM) | As long as the contact | Deleted with the contact | Record of the business relationship |
| Meetings with contacts | As long as the contact | The link and the person's address are removed when the contact is deleted | Planning and record of meetings |
| Call transcripts | 12 months (setting) | Deleted | Accurate notes and follow ups; can be deleted sooner at any time |
| News items about companies | 12 months (setting) | Deleted | Timely reasons to get in touch |
| Do not contact list (hashed email or phone, masked hint, reason) | For as long as Moca contacts people | Reviewed if marketing stops altogether | Needed so an objection keeps working |
| Deals and their history | [To decide: for example 6 years after closing, in line with accounting records] | [Delete or anonymise] | [Commercial and financial records] |
| Team members' accounts | While they use the CRM | Switched off when they leave; [delete after N months] | Access and accountability |
| Audit log (views, downloads, important changes) | [To decide: for example 2 years] | [Delete] | Security and accountability |
| Requests about data and their outcome | [To decide: for example 2 years after closing] | [Delete] | Showing requests were handled properly |
| Breach records | [To decide: for example 6 years] | [Delete] | Showing breaches were handled properly (required even when not reported) |
| Database backups | 14 days, encrypted | Deleted automatically | Recovery after a failure; deleted records disappear from backups within 14 days |
| Knowledge library documents | Until removed by an admin or manager | Deleted | Background for the AI; documents with personal details are never sent to it |

Items in [square brackets] are not yet set in the CRM and need a decision. The CRM currently keeps them until someone removes them.
