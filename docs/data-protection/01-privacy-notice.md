## Who we are

We are [Moca's registered company name], trading as Moca ("Moca", "we", "us"). We are registered in England and Wales, company number [number], at [registered address]. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number [number].

For anything about this notice or your details, contact our data protection lead at [privacy email address], or write to us at [postal address].

## Who this notice is for

People who work at businesses and public bodies we may work with: prospective customers, customers, partners and their staff. It explains how we use your details when we contact you about Moca's energy software for commercial property.

## What we hold about you

Only business details:

* your name and job title
* your work email address and work phone number
* the organisation you work for
* a link to your public professional profile, if you have one
* notes about our business conversations with you, such as what you told us about your organisation's energy plans
* emails and meetings between you and our team, and notes or recordings of sales calls (see "Calls" below)
* where we got your details, and when
* whether you have asked us not to contact you

We do not collect sensitive information about you, such as health, political opinions, religion or anything about your family.

## Where we got your details

Either from you, or from a public or business source such as your organisation's website, a professional networking site, an industry event or directory, or a colleague who introduced us. We record the source and date for every contact. If we got your details from someone other than you, we tell you who we are and how to object in our first message, and within one month at the latest.

## Why we use your details, and our lawful basis

We use your details to contact you about how Moca could help your organisation, and to manage our business relationship with you. Our lawful basis is **legitimate interests**: our interest in finding and serving business customers, balanced against your rights. We have written an assessment of this balance, which you can ask to see.

If you are a sole trader or in some types of partnership, the law treats you as an individual for marketing emails, and we only email you if you have agreed.

## Calls

We may record sales calls, and make a written transcript, so our notes are accurate and we follow up properly. We will always tell you at the start of a recorded call, and you can ask us not to record. Transcripts are kept for [12] months and then deleted, and you can ask us to delete one sooner.

## Use of AI

We use an AI service to help our team draft emails, summarise news about organisations and summarise call transcripts. Before anything is sent to the AI, email addresses and phone numbers are removed. A person always reviews what the AI writes, and nothing is sent to you automatically. We do not make decisions about you by automated means alone: our scoring is about organisations, not people. [Confirm: our AI provider does not use our data to train its models.]

## Who we share your details with

We do not sell your details. They are processed for us by suppliers who store or handle data on our behalf under contracts that protect it:

* [Hostinger], which hosts our systems, in [location]
* [Anthropic], our AI provider, in [location]
* [email sending service], for our own team's alert emails
* Google or Microsoft, where our team's email and calendar accounts are hosted

Where a supplier stores data outside the UK, we make sure there are safeguards the law recognises, such as a UK adequacy decision or the international data transfer agreement. [Confirm per supplier.]

## Marketing emails and calls

Every marketing email we send includes a link to stop further emails. If you ask us to stop, we stop straight away, for everyone at Moca. We keep a minimal record (a scrambled copy of your email address or phone number) so that we never contact you again, even if the rest of your details are deleted.

Before calling a business phone number, we check it against the Telephone Preference Service and Corporate Telephone Preference Service, and we do not call numbers registered there unless you have told us we can.

## How long we keep your details

* Contact details: deleted after [24] months with no activity between us.
* Call transcripts: [12] months.
* The minimal do not contact record: for as long as we contact people, so your request keeps working.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

* tell you what we hold about you and send you a copy
* correct anything that is wrong
* delete your details
* limit how we use them
* stop using them, including for marketing (we will always do this for marketing)
* give you a copy in a format another organisation can use

Contact [privacy email address]. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm who you are. There is normally no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have used your details, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, or 0303 123 1113.

## Changes

We will update this notice if how we use your details changes, and show the date of the latest version at the top.
