# Staff guidance: notes, opt outs and the do not call registers

**Draft for legal review.** For everyone at Moca who uses the CRM. It takes five minutes to read.

## Why this matters

The CRM holds details about real people. Anyone can ask to see everything we hold about them, including your notes. Write every note as if the person will read it, because they might.

## Notes: what to record

**Do record** business facts that help the relationship:

* their role and responsibilities, and who else is involved in decisions
* their organisation's energy plans, buildings, targets and deadlines
* what was agreed, what was promised, and when to follow up
* objections or concerns about the business case

**Do not record:**

* personal opinions about the person ("difficult", "not very bright")
* anything about their health, family, religion, politics, sexuality, ethnicity or trade union membership, even if they mention it
* personal contact details (personal mobile, home address, personal email)
* gossip, or what others said about them

If someone shares something personal, leave it out of the CRM.

## Where details come from

Use business sources only: their organisation's website, professional profiles, industry directories and events, or introductions. Always record the source when adding a contact. Do not buy lists without checking with the data protection lead.

## First contact

Every marketing email from the CRM includes our details, a privacy line and an unsubscribe link automatically. Do not remove it or send marketing from outside the CRM. The first email to someone whose details we found elsewhere must reach them within one month of us getting their details; the contact's page shows whether this has been done.

## Opt outs

* If anyone says they do not want to hear from us, in any words and by any route, **opt them out straight away** on their contact page. It applies to everyone at Moca immediately.
* Never add them back, re import them, or contact them from another system. The CRM keeps a scrambled record so they stay blocked.
* If they are unsure or ask what we hold, pass it to the data protection lead as a request.

## Sole traders and partnerships

These are treated as individuals for marketing emails, so we need their agreement first. Set the organisation type correctly on the contact; the CRM blocks marketing emails until consent is recorded.

## Cold calls and the do not call registers

* Before cold calling a number, it must be checked against the **TPS** (Telephone Preference Service) and **CTPS** (Corporate Telephone Preference Service), and the result and date recorded on the contact.
* A check is valid for 28 days; the CRM asks for a new one after that.
* Do not call a registered number unless the person has told us they are happy to be called.

## Calls

Always give the recording notice before recording (see the call recording script). If someone says no, do not record.

## AI drafts

AI written emails, scripts and summaries are drafts. Read and correct every one before using it. Check facts and figures against the source; if the AI says something you cannot confirm, remove it.

## Security

* Sign in only with your Moca work account. Never share it.
* Lock your screen when you step away.
* Download lists only when needed, delete them afterwards, and never send them to personal accounts.
* Report anything that goes wrong to the data protection lead straight away, however small (see the breach procedure). Reporting quickly is never a problem.
