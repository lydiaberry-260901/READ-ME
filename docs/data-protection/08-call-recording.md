# Call recording notice and script

**Draft for legal review.** For everyone at Moca who records sales calls.

## The rule

Before recording any call, tell the other person **that it is being recorded, and why**, and give them the chance to say no. If they say no, do not record, and take notes by hand instead. Then record in the CRM, on the call's page, whether they were told (the CRM flags any call where this is missing).

## At the start of a phone or video call

> "Before we start, I'd like to record this call so I can keep accurate notes and follow up properly. The recording is kept securely and deleted after [12] months. Is that all right with you?"

If they agree:

> "Thank you. You can ask me to stop recording at any time."

If they say no:

> "That's absolutely fine, I won't record. I'll just take notes."

## In a meeting invitation (optional, as well as saying it)

> We may record this call to keep accurate notes, with your agreement at the start. Recordings and transcripts are kept securely for [12] months. You can say no, or ask us to stop at any time. More about how we use your details: [privacy notice link]

## If someone asks later

* **To see it:** treat it as a request about their data (see the procedure).
* **To delete it:** open the call in the CRM (Calls) and delete the transcript, then tell them it has been done.

## For call recording tools that join calls automatically

Check the tool announces itself or shows a clear recording sign, and still give the notice above yourself. Set the tool to send "recordingNoticeGiven: true" only when the notice was actually given.
